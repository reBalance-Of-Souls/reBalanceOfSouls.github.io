Patch V1.1.2

August 2026 · Komamura's overhaul, Aizen's mobility pass & a punish-consistency sweep
Patch Highlights
Balance

Komamura's Keep-Away Curbed

His zoning Signature sat around 36 frames plus on block. Its cancel timing has been roughly doubled so it now lands about 13 frames minus, with extended Tilt armor and a much healthier Weakened state alongside it.
System

Kyōka Suigetsu Fixed

Aizen's kill denial ran 700 frames with no input lock and no invulnerability, leaving him exposed on arrival. Both now cover the full cutscene, and the move no longer charges 2 Reverse bars on top of its flames.
Damage

Ichibei's SP1 Pass

Heavy SP1 hits noticeably harder in both stages, Light SP1 can combo in Awakening (once), and Tilt links at any range — while the counter loses its combo entirely.
Game Modes

Extra Konpaku & Sudden Death

Two new modes at opposite ends of the dial: Extra Konpaku pushes every character to 12 Konpaku for long sets, while Sudden Death gives everyone a single one.
Hotfixes

Bug Fix Sweep

Grimmjow's Dash Light no longer deals 6000 damage instead of 125, Yhwach's Sankt Zwinger no longer runs the game at nine times speed, and Byakuya loses two whiff-cancel exploits.
System

Punish Consistency

A sweep across Soi Fon, Ulquiorra and Kisuke: raw Hoho recovery is retuned for consistent punishing, and Kisuke's Byakurai beam no longer stays live through his own movement options.

Developer's Notes
Two modes sitting at opposite ends of the same dial. Extra Konpaku stretches a set out far enough that comeback mechanics and resource management actually matter across a long match, while Sudden Death compresses everything into a single decisive exchange. The per-character Konpaku counts in Extra Konpaku follow each character's existing balance rather than being flat across the roster.

Developer's Notes
Komamura's Signature was doing all of its work from a distance. The zoning version sat around 36 frames plus on block, which meant he could claim a screen's worth of space and never really be challenged for holding it, so its cancel timing has been roughly doubled and it now lands around 13 frames minus instead. The payoff stays on the Forward Heavy Ender, which hits considerably harder, so the move is still worth committing to when it actually leads somewhere. The rest of the pass is about making his armor and his weaker state honest. Tilt's Super Armor now runs through the move's active frames in both Initial Stage and Weakened, so committing to it is no longer beaten by a simple mash, while SP1 hits far harder in both states and keeps its identity as an armored wake-up and whiff punish that stays unsafe on block. Weakened picks up a much healthier pool of health along with a Dash Light that finally matches the Initial Stage version instead of sitting minus on block and short on hitstun.

Developer's Notes
Kyōka Suigetsu was the outstanding problem. Its cutscene runs 700 frames and carried neither an input lock nor invulnerability, so Aizen came out of his own kill denial actionable but completely exposed, and any input buffered before the denial would fire the moment he arrived. Dash Light is the other change, with its forward travel cut by a quarter so it reads less as a telegraphed commitment and more as something he can genuinely open with, while still being steppable.

Developer's Notes
Yellow Reverse was building resources a little too freely, so its passive Fighting Spirit gain has been removed and its Spiritual Power gain rate was reduced to stop it from being the do all wonder action that it was. The goal is to leave the two Reverse options feeling like real alternatives rather than one being the default optimal option.

Developer's Notes
The community has always been divided on Byakuya and how to properly balance him. His ability to zone unchecked left a lot feeling like he was just unfair or too strong, but nerfing too much of his kit would only gut the character. That's why Byakuya's Awakening has been rebuilt around form-switching: Senkei for melee, Scattered State for ranged, with SP1 and SP2 acting as the switches with SP2 even starts a combo on top of swapping. His new command-grab Signature allows him a much stronger mix-up tool that combos into hoho follow up, Yellow Reverse, or SP1. In base form, Scattered State SP1 was a long-range instant whiff punish that could combo while also being plus on block made it too strong of a move, so it is now minus.
